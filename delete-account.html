Delete your FitRoutine account

FitRoutine (com.ksil.fitroutine)

You can permanently delete your FitRoutine account and its data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open FitRoutine and go to More → the profile button.
  2. Make sure you are signed in to the account you want to remove.
  3. Tap Delete account and data.
  4. Confirm when asked. The account and its stored data are deleted right away.

Request deletion without the app

If you can no longer sign in or have uninstalled the app, email ksil.apps@gmail.com from the address on the account with the subject “Delete my FitRoutine account”.

Requests are actioned within 30 days. We may need to confirm you control the email address before deleting, so that nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your profile, including any display name and email address stored with it.
  • Your synced workout plans, workout sessions, completed sets, exercise history, and app settings.

What happens on your device

Deleting from inside the app also removes the copy of that account's data stored on that device. If you used FitRoutine on more than one device, open the app on each one and sign out, or uninstall it, to clear the local copy there as well.

Workouts you recorded as a guest, before ever signing in, are stored only on your device. They are not part of your account and are removed by uninstalling the app.

Retention

Deleted account records are removed from the live database immediately. Encrypted infrastructure backups may retain residual copies for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten. We do not restore deleted accounts from those backups.
